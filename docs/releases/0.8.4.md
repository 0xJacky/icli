- `app refresh`, `sb uicache` and `app register` are fast. They listed every app LaunchServices knows with its plug-ins and entitlements, a refresh three times and a register twice, and reading each app's plug-ins took most of it: on an iPhone SE with iOS 15.8.6 a refresh with nothing to do took 1.3 s. Apps are now listed from LaunchServices' application records, which take a few milliseconds, with proxies as the fallback where those are unavailable, and what a registration sets is read only for the apps the refresh plans. The same refresh takes 0.33 s on the SE and 0.03 s on an iOS 26.6.2 vphone (0.53 s before); a register on the SE takes about 0.7 s instead of 1.2 to 1.5 s. What is compared with uicache's registration is unchanged.
- `screen shot --native-resolution` captures the boot logo and the respring spinner at their real size. While SpringBoard was not running, UIKit's capture came out magnified by the screen scale, so on a 2x screen the image held the top-left quarter of the boot logo; icli now captures through the render server until SpringBoard answers. A quarter-turned fallback screenshot of a non-square display is no longer shifted out of its frame. Contributed by @0xJacky in #6.
- A host of IcliKit outside the bootstrap, such as vphoned on the system volume, finds the RootHide root in `/var/containers/Bundle/Application` (`jailbreak.source` `filesystem .jbroot`), and the package commands read dpkg's database from `Library/dpkg` when `var/lib/dpkg` is missing, as on a bootstrap that has not installed the dpkg package yet. Contributed by @0xJacky in #7.
